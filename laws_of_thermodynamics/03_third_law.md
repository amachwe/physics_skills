# Third Law of Thermodynamics
The Third Law of Thermodynamics relates to the behavior of systems as they approach absolute zero temperature (0 Kelvin).

It states that the entropy of a perfect crystal at absolute zero is exactly equal to all zero. 
$$S \rightarrow 0 \text{ as } T \rightarrow 0$$

This law implies that it is practically impossible to reach absolute zero in practice, as an infinite number of steps would be required to remove heat from a system as it approaches the limit. It provides a reference point for calculating absolute entropy.
