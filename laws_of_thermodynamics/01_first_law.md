# First Law of Thermodynamics
The First Law of Thermodynamics is a version of the law of conservation of energy, specifically applied to thermodynamics systems.

It states that energy can be transformed from one form to another, but it cannot be created or destroyed. In mathematical terms:
$$\Delta U = Q - W$$
Where:
- $\Delta U$ is the change in internal energy of the system.
- $Q$ is the heat added to the system.
- $W$ is the work done by the system.

This law establishes that the total energy of an isolated system remains constant.
