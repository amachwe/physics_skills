# Second Law of Thermodynamics
The Second Law of Thermodynamics deals with the direction of spontaneous processes and the concept of entropy.

Key principles include:
1. **Entropy Increase**: The total entropy of an isolated system can never decrease over time; it can only remain constant or increase. $\Delta S \ge 0$.
2. **Heat Flow**: Heat spontaneously flows from a higher temperature object to a lower temperature object.
3. **Efficiency**: No heat engine can be 100% efficient because some energy is always lost to the environment as waste heat.

Entropy is a measure of the "disorder" or number of possible microscopic configurations of a system.
