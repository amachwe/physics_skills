# Summary of the Laws of Thermodynamics

The laws of thermodynamics describe how energy moves and changes form in the universe.

- **Zeroth Law**: Establishes temperature as a measurable property.
- **First Law**: Conservation of energy ($\Delta U = Q - W$).
- **Second Law**: Entropy always increases or stays constant; heat flows from hot to cold.
- **Third Law**: Absolute zero is the limit where entropy reaches a minimum (zero for perfect crystals).

Together, these laws provide the fundamental framework for understanding heat, work, and energy in physical systems.
