# Applications and Examples

## Important Categories
- **Set**: The category of sets and functions.
- **Grp**: The category of groups and group homomorphisms.
- **Vect**: The category of vector spaces and linear transformations.
- **Top**: The category of topological spaces and continuous maps.

## Concepts in Practice
- **Functorial Semantics**: Mapping the structure of a category to properties.
- **Algebraic Structures**: Defining groups, rings, and modules using universal properties in terms of limits/colimits.
