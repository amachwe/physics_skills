# Limits and Colimits

## Limits
A limit is a way to construct an object from a diagram in a category. Common examples include:
- **Products**: $A \times B$
- **Equalizers**
- **Pullbacks**

## Colimits
Colimits are the dual of limits (moving "outward" rather than "inward"). Common examples:
- **Coproducts** (e.g., $A \oplus B$)
- **Coequalizers**
- **Pushouts**

Limits and colimits allow us to define constructions that are coordinate-independent.
