# Quantum Numbers

Quantum numbers are numerical values used to describe the quantum state of an electron in an atom. They define the size, shape, and orientation of orbitals.

## The Four Quantum Numbers:
1. **Principal Quantum Number ($n$)**: Determines the main energy level and shell (e.g., $n = 1, 2, 3 \dots$).
2. **Angular Momentum Quantum Number ($l$)**: Determines the shape of the orbital (e.g., $s, p, d, f$ orbitals).
3. **Magnetic Quantum Number ($m_l$)**: Describes the orientation of the orbital in space.
4. **Spin Quantum Number ($m_s$)**: Indicates the intrinsic spin of the electron ($\pm 1/2$).
