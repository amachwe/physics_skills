# Wave-Particle Duality

Quantum mechanics posits that every particle or quantum entity may be described as either a particle or a wave. This is one of the fundamental concepts of quantum mechanics.

## Key Concepts:
* **Wave Properties**: Particles like electrons exhibit wave-like behavior (diffraction, interference).
* **Particle Properties**: Entities such as photons behave like discrete packets of energy (particles).
* **De Broglie Hypothesis**: Every moving particle has a wave associated with it ($ \lambda = h/p $).
* **Photoelectric Effect**: Evidence for the particle nature of light.
