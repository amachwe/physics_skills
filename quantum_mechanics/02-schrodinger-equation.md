# Schrodinger Equation

The Schrödinger equation is the fundamental governing equation of wave mechanics in quantum mechanics. It describes how the wavefunction of a quantum system changes over time or space.

## Key Elements:
* **Time-Dependent Equation**: $i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\s} \Psi(\mathbf{r}, t) = \hat{H} \Psi(\mathbf{r}, t)$
* **Time-Independent Form**: $\hat{H} \psi = E \psi$ (where $E$ is the energy eigenvalue).
* **Wavefunction ($\psi$)**: A mathematical function that contains all information about the state of the system. The probability density is given by $|\psi|^2$.
* **Hamiltonian Operator ($\hat{H}$)**: Represents the total energy of the system (Kinetic + Potential).
