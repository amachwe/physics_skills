# Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle

The uncertainty principle is a fundamental limit on the precision with which certain pairs of physical properties (complementary variables) can be known simultaneously.

## Core Principles:
* **Position and Momentum**: $\Delta x \cdot \Delta p \ge \frac{\hbar}{2}$. Measuring one more accurately increases the uncertainty of the other.
* **Energy and Time**: $\Delta E \cdot \Delta t \ge \frac{\hbar}{2}$.
* **Origin**: It is not a failure of measurement technology but an inherent property of quantum systems (wave nature).
