# Superposition and Entanglement

## Superposition
A quantum system can exist in multiple states simultaneously until a measurement is made. 
* **Equation**: $\lvert \psi \rangle = \alpha\lvert 0 \rangle + \beta\lvert 1 \rangle$
* **Measurement**: Collapses the wave function into one of the basis states.

## Entanglement
A phenomenon where two or more particles become linked such that the quantum state of each particle cannot be described independently.
* **Non-locality**: Changes to one particle instantaneously affect the state of its entangled partner, regardless of distance.
* **Bell's Theorem**: Shows that no local hidden variable theory can reproduce all predictions of quantum mechanics.
