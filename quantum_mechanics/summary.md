# Summary of Quantum Mechanics

Quantum mechanics is the theory of the behavior of matter and energy at atomic and subatomic scales. 

Key takeaways include:
- **Wave-Particle Duality**: Matter behaves like both particles and waves.
- **Uncertainty Principle**: Fundamental limits on precision of certain pairs of properties.
- **Schrödinger Equation**: The primary equation for calculating the evolution of quantum states.
- **Superposition & Entanglement**: Core features allowing for phenomena like quantum computing and teleportation.
- **Quantum Numbers**: Define the discrete allowed states of electrons in atoms.

The transition from classical mechanics to quantum mechanics marks a shift from determinism to probability-based descriptions of nature.
